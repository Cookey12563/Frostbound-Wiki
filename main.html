What is Frostbound?

Frostbound is a story-driven web series that originally started in Super Mario Maker 2.

The series follows a group of heroes as they face a mysterious frozen threat, uncover secrets, and learn about friendship, trust, and the choices they make along the way.

How can I read Frostbound?

Currently, you can read Frostbound through Super Mario Maker 2. Later, it will also be available in regular comic form.
