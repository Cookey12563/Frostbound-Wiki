Lore

FROSTBOUND takes place in a world where an unnatural winter has begun to spread from the influence of a mysterious figure known as Snow. As the frozen landscape expands, a towering ice castle appears, becoming the center of the growing threat. The castle is not a natural formation—it was deliberately created and serves as the source of Snow's control over the surrounding area.

The story follows a group of friends led by Emerald as they investigate the strange snowfall and eventually enter Snow's domain. What first appears to be an abandoned frozen fortress is revealed to be an active stronghold filled with traps, hidden mechanisms, and deliberate attempts to separate and eliminate intruders. The castle itself reflects Snow's will, with hazards designed to force trespassers deeper into its halls.

One of the castle's caretakers is Modern, a young trapkeeper responsible for maintaining many of the castle's mechanisms. Although Modern initially remains loyal to Snow out of fear, he gradually realizes that Snow's actions have gone far beyond what he can accept. His decision to stand with Emerald and the others becomes one of the story's first major turning points.

Snow possesses powerful ice magic capable of creating weapons, manipulating the environment, and inflicting wounds that ordinary treatment cannot fully heal. This magic is repeatedly used to maintain control over the castle and to attack anyone who opposes Snow's plans.

In contrast, Frost discovers a different application of ice magic. Rather than harming others, Frost awakens the ability to heal injuries and remove the lingering effects of Snow's frost-burn. This discovery establishes a direct contrast between Snow's destructive use of ice and Frost's restorative abilities.

As the group continues their journey, they encounter both physical dangers and emotional challenges. Moments of sacrifice, trust, and forgiveness become just as important as surviving the castle itself. Characters repeatedly face choices that define who they become, making personal decisions a central part of the story alongside the conflict against Snow.

Throughout the series, themes of found family, trust, healing versus control, and choice remain at the heart of the narrative. Rather than focusing solely on defeating an enemy, FROSTBOUND explores how compassion, loyalty, and the decisions people make shape both themselves and the world around them.

Although the group's immediate goal is to stop Snow and survive the frozen castle, the events of the story suggest that the growing winter is only part of a much larger conflict still unfolding.
